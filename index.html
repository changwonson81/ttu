Human Factors & Safety Researcher

Making complex systems safer, smarter, and more human-centered.

I am a faculty member in Industrial, Manufacturing, and Systems Engineering at Texas Tech University. My research examines how people understand, coordinate with, and adapt to complex technologies in high-stakes environments.

My work connects human factors, cognitive systems engineering, safety science, and resilience engineering to improve human–AI teaming, emergency response, healthcare, and industrial operations.
